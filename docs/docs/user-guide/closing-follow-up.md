# Closing a Follow-up

Ending the video call does **not** close the file. The follow-up stays open so
you can keep chatting, share a report, or schedule the next appointment.

Close it once you are done with the beneficiary.

## Export before you close

Click **Export PDF** on the follow-up page to download the file: the details,
the appointments and the chat history. Do it before the retention delay expires
— once the follow-up is deleted, nothing can be recovered.

## Close the follow-up

1. Open the follow-up
2. Click **Close Follow-up**
3. Confirm

The file becomes read-only, moves to the **Closed** tab, and disappears from the
*To handle* list.

If your administrator configured automatic deletion, the confirmation dialog
tells you how long the file is kept — *It will be automatically deleted after X
hour(s)*. The countdown starts when you close the file, not when the call ends.

!!! warning "Deletion is permanent"
    Once the delay has elapsed, the follow-up, its messages, its attachments and
    the temporary guests attached to it are removed for good. Export the PDF and
    save any document you need in your own records first.

## Reopen a follow-up

As long as it has not been deleted, a closed follow-up can be reopened from its
page with **Reopen Follow-up**. The chat and the appointments become available
again, and the deletion countdown stops.

## Automatic closing

Some follow-ups close on their own:

- follow-ups created automatically to carry the chat of a standalone appointment
  close a while after the join window ends
- temporary guest accounts with no upcoming appointment are removed
  automatically, when your administrator enabled that option

Files you created by hand are never closed automatically — that decision is
yours.
